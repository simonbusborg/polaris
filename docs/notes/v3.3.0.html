Polaris 3.3.0

October 29, 2026

  • Credential expiry — a Data Portal credential lives 90 days, and Polaris now keeps the date: shown under Settings → Data Portal and correctable there, a reminder a week before and on the day with a button to the portal, and a failure row that says "expired" instead of "rejected". Credentials saved before this version get a date counted from when they were last saved; check it, since the real one may be earlier.
  • Who the Data Portal is for — Settings → Data Portal now says the portal is open only to owners in the EU/EEA, and that elsewhere Polaris keeps reading the car through the login.
