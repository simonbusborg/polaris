Polaris 2.10.0

September 10, 2026

  • Choose how often the car is polled — a "Refresh while parked" setting in the menu bar pane: every 1, 2, 5, 10 or 15 minutes, applied the moment it's changed. Charging and driving still refresh every minute — those are the moments the numbers actually move.
  • The menu speaks VoiceOver — the data rows, the car image and the widget now carry proper accessibility labels, so "Battery, 78%" is read aloud instead of silence. Decorative pieces like the battery bar stay quiet rather than repeating what the text next to them already says.
  • Readable release notes in the update window — Sparkle's update panel now shows what a release means for you, written from this roadmap, instead of raw commit subjects.
  • No more half-translated greeting — Chinese and Korean systems got a localized "hello" atop an otherwise English menu; the greeting now only speaks languages the rest of the app speaks too.
